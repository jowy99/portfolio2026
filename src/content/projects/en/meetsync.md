- Coordinates meetups and trips
- Everyone marks days and places
- Prototype on hold, unpublished

## Goals

- Mark availability on a calendar
- Choose places and add comments
- Respond without creating an account

## Challenges

- Editing a response without signing up
- Clearly warning if the database fails

## Technical decisions

- Next.js and React with TypeScript
- PostgreSQL with Drizzle
- Server-side validation
- Anonymous identity in the browser

- Availability calendar
